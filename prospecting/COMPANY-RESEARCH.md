## Executive Summary

WethosAI is a seed-stage AI platform founded in 2023 and headquartered in Newport Beach, California. The company builds what it calls an "Enterprise Cognition Platform" — using behavioral science, NLP, and continuous profiling to model how individuals and teams actually think, decide, and collaborate under pressure. The product goes beyond point-in-time personality assessments (MBTI, DiSC) to deliver what WethosAI calls Aii™ (Artificial Individual Intelligence): living behavioral blueprints that adapt as teams evolve. Key modules include Wethos Styles, Copilot, XO (an AI Chief of Staff), Teams, and a Comfort Index for burnout monitoring.

The founding team is unusually credentialed for a seed company. CEO Stuart McClure co-founded Cylance, which sold to BlackBerry for $1.5B — one of the fastest companies on record to reach $100M ARR. The $7.5M seed round (August 2024) was led by GFT Ventures and included Capellas Partners, Cove Fund, Miramar Digital Ventures, and NOMO Ventures. With only ~22 employees and an estimated $2.5M ARR, the company is early but has strong pedigree and a differentiated technical angle.

As a sales prospect, WethosAI is in active build mode: limited budget relative to a post-Series A company, small buying committee, but fast decision-making and a CEO with a track record of scaling fast and spending on the tools that matter. Best suited for vendors with a short time-to-value story, a credible enterprise proof point, and a clear behavioral-science or team performance angle.

## 1. Company Overview

WethosAI was spun out of NumberOne AI, an AI incubator founded in 2022 that builds startups in Newport Beach. It occupies the intersection of behavioral science, organizational psychology, and enterprise AI — a space it calls "Enterprise Cognition." The company's philosophy is **"Humans + AI > AI"**, with the stated tagline *"Ensuring Human Relevancy in an AI World."*

Mission (per company): *"To empower every human being to discover their unique cognitive signature and leverage AI as a force multiplier — creating a world where AI doesn't replace humans, but amplifies creativity, emotional intelligence, and collaboration."*

The company targets Boards of Directors and CxOs specifically — positioning itself as a strategic C-suite tool rather than an HR or team-manager product. This is a deliberate top-down GTM motion.
