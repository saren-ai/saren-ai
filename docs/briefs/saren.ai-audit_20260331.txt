# saren.ai Schema Audit — Summary

**Overall Score: 27 / 100** *(vs. halcyon.ai's 36/100)*

### Key Context
Since saren.ai is a personal brand site, the anchor entity shifts from `Organization` to **`Person`** — but the same five-tier logic applies. The biggest structural story here: the FAQPage tier is actually *stronger* than halcyon.ai's, while tiers 2, 3, and 4 are completely absent.

---

### Tier 1 — Person Anchor Entity (48/100) ⚠️ Partial
The `Person` schema is deployed on **every single page** — that's a genuine positive. Name, URL, image, jobTitle, description, and 4 `sameAs` links (LinkedIn, Bluesky, Instagram, Mastodon) are all present. However, the schema is **identical across all 18 pages** with no page-specific adaptation, there is no `@id` anchor (the single highest-impact missing element), and critical enrichment fields are absent — no `alumniOf` (Cylance/BlackBerry are mentioned in prose but not structured), no `knowsAbout`, no `worksFor`, no `hasOccupation`. There's also a **positioning mismatch**: the `jobTitle` reads "VP, Demand Generation — Cybersecurity & AI" (a past corporate role), while the site's actual positioning is "Fractional CMO & AI Operations Consultant."

### Tier 2 — WebSite + WebPage (0/100) ❌ Completely Absent
Zero `WebSite` schemas, zero `WebPage` schemas, zero page subtypes on any of the 18 pages audited. Additionally — and this is a separate but compounding technical SEO issue — **no page on the site has a canonical URL tag**. This is sitewide, not just a schema gap.

### Tier 3 — BreadcrumbList (0/100) ❌ Completely Absent
No breadcrumb schema anywhere, and no DOM-level breadcrumb navigation either. This is particularly impactful because the site has a meaningful nested hierarchy: `/portfolio` → `/portfolio/b2b-marketing-framework`, and `/about` → `/about/clients`. Without breadcrumbs, Google has no structured signal about these parent-child relationships.

### Tier 4 — Article / CreativeWork + Person (0/100) ❌ Completely Absent
This is the biggest E-E-A-T gap on the site. The entire portfolio — ROI Simulator, B2B Marketing Framework, 10-Touch Sales Play, 120-Day Content Journey, Sovereign Personas, etc. — is Saren's primary evidence of expertise. **None of it is attributed to her in schema.** No `CreativeWork`, no `Article`, no `author` linkages. Google cannot structurally connect these works to Saren as an entity. The `/thinking` section also has zero content-type schema.

### Tier 5 — FAQPage / HowTo / Service (55/100) ⚠️ Best Tier on Site
This is where saren.ai meaningfully outperforms halcyon.ai. FAQPage schemas with well-populated Q&A are present on the `/about`, `/contact`, and most `/portfolio/*` pages — including pages with 6–7 substantive questions. This is a real AEO/AI-citation signal that's working. The gap is the absence of `HowTo` schema on methodology pages (B2B Framework, 10-Touch Play, 120-Day Journey) and `Service`/`Offer` schema on service-oriented pages.

### Top 3 Quick Wins
1. **Add `@id: "https://saren.ai/#person"`** to the root Person schema — unlocks cross-schema entity linkage across the entire site
2. **Add canonical tags sitewide** — foundational technical SEO, no schema required, immediate fix
3. **Add `CreativeWork` schema with `author: { "@id": "https://saren.ai/#person" }`** to every portfolio page — the most direct E-E-A-T lever available
