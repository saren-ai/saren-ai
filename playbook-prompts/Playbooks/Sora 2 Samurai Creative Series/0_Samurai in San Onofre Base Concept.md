# Sora 2 Prompt 0 - Samurai in San Onofre


Prose scene description: A young traditional samurai, 24 years old, stands confidently on a classic wooden surfboard, dressed in full traditional attire—richly patterned kimono, wide hakama pants, and a tied obi belt, his katana sheathed at his side. He carves skillfully across a rolling wave at San Onofre, California, moving smoothly from left to right. The ocean sparkles under a vibrant, colorful setting sun that blazes warm hues of orange, pink, and purple across the sky and reflects on the curling water. The scene blends serene natural beauty with intense focus and skilled motion of the samurai, embodying grace and tradition set against the coast’s rugged shoreline.
Cinematography:
Camera shot: wide establishing shot capturing the coastline and the surfer from slightly below eye level, tracking right to follow his carving line.
Mood: cinematic and serene, with warm glowing light emphasizing the harmony between ancient tradition and modern freedom.
Actions:
	•	The samurai gracefully carves the wave’s face, water droplets spraying skyward.
	•	He shifts weight, leaning into the turn, steady focus on the horizon.
	•	The setting sun dips lower, casting a colorful silhouette glow around him.
Dialogue:
No dialogue, focus on natural ocean sounds and subtle wind noise.
Background Sound:
Ocean waves crashing, gentle sea breeze, distant cries of seabirds, subtle rustle of the samurai’s kimono fabric.
