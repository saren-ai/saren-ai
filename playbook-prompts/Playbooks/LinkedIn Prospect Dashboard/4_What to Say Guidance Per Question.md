### Q1 — Buying Authority by Title

Be explicit about who actually owns the decision. Seniority, function, and org size all matter here.

> *"VP of Sales, CRO, or Head of Revenue at B2B SaaS companies between 100–1,000 employees. Director-level is fine if they own the budget. I'm not interested in ops or enablement titles unless they're explicitly running the tech stack."*

### Q2 — Industry and Intent Signals

Name the verticals, then flag what buying signals look like in your space.

> *"SaaS, fintech, and GTM tech. Top of the list: anyone who's posted about pipeline efficiency, outbound motion, or scaling their sales team. Bonus points if they've recently changed roles, hired SDRs, or publicly talked about CRM consolidation."*

### Q3 — Your Outreach Voice

Don't say "professional but friendly." Actually describe how you write.

> *"Casual and direct. Short sentences. I open with something specific to them, not a compliment. I never say 'I hope this finds you well' or 'I wanted to reach out.' No buzzwords. The message should read like a peer who did their homework, not a rep who's working a sequence."*

### Q4 — Conversion Goal

Be specific about the ask and the framing.

> *"A 20-minute call, no pitch deck. I'm qualifying fit, not selling. The message should make it easy to say yes — low friction, clear value in the conversation, not the product."*
