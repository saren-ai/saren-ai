Turn your LinkedIn connections export into a ranked prospect shortlist, personalized outreach messages, a CRM-ready CSV, and a custom HTML dashboard — all inside Claude.

LinkedIn → **Me** (top right) → **Settings & Privacy** → **Data Privacy** → **Get a copy of your data** → select **Connections** → **Request archive**

LinkedIn will email you a download link within 10–30 minutes (occasionally up to 24 hours). Download the ZIP, unzip it, grab `Connections.csv`.

That's the only file you need.
