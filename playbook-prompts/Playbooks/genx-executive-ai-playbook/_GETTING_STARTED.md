# Getting Started

This guide gets you from "just bought this" to "running prompts in production" in under 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Pick Your LLM

Every prompt in this playbook works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other capable chat-based LLM — none of the prompts depend on a specific provider's features. Pick whichever one you already have access to and trust with your documents (check your organization's data-handling policy before pasting internal materials into a third-party tool).

## Step 2: Run Your First Prompt

**Recommended starter:** [1.1 The Pre-Meeting Oracle](https://github.com/saren-ai/genx-ai-prompts/blob/main/executive_ai_playbook/1.1_The_Pre-Meeting_Oracle.md)

### Quick Version:

1. Open your LLM
2. Go to [1.1 The Pre-Meeting Oracle](https://github.com/saren-ai/genx-ai-prompts/blob/main/executive_ai_playbook/1.1_The_Pre-Meeting_Oracle.md)
3. Copy the entire prompt
4. Paste it in, then replace the `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` with your actual documents
5. Hit enter

## Step 3: Understand the Placeholder System

Every prompt in this playbook uses `{{DOUBLE_CURLY_PLACEHOLDERS}}` to mark where your specific input goes — things like `{{DOCUMENTS}}`, `{{DECISION}}`, or `{{CONTEXT}}`. Each prompt's "How to use" section tells you exactly which placeholders it expects and what to replace them with.

A few tips that apply across all 20 prompts:
- Replace the placeholder text entirely — don't leave the `{{ }}` markers in what you send.
- More specific input produces more specific output. A vague paste of "our Q3 numbers" gets a vague answer; the actual deck plus the actual memo gets you something usable.
- If a prompt asks for more than one input (e.g. both `{{DECISION}}` and `{{CONTEXT}}`), fill in both — the reasoning step built into each prompt depends on having the full picture.
