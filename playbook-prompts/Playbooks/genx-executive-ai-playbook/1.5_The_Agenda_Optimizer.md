# 1.5 Agenda Optimizer: Cut Meeting Time by 30%

## What this does
Analyzes your proposed agenda items against quarterly objectives and redesigns your meeting structure for maximum strategic impact. Determines what to cut, what to handle async, and what deserves deep discussion, with time allocations based on decision criticality.

## How to use
1. Copy the entire prompt below
2. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred LLM
3. Replace `{{Q3_OBJECTIVES}}` with your quarterly or current strategic objectives
4. Replace `{{AGENDA_ITEMS}}` with your proposed agenda items
5. Hit enter

---

## The Prompt
```
You are a strategic meeting consultant tasked with optimizing a weekly executive meeting for maximum strategic impact. You will analyze proposed agenda items against quarterly objectives and redesign the meeting structure.

Here are the quarterly objectives:
<q3_objectives>
{{Q3_OBJECTIVES}}
</q3_objectives>

Here are the proposed agenda items for the weekly executive meeting:
<agenda_items>
{{AGENDA_ITEMS}}
</agenda_items>

Complete the following tasks in order:
1. Assess each agenda item's alignment with the objectives above
2. Rate each item's decision criticality (high/medium/low)
3. Determine whether each item requires real-time executive discussion or can be handled asynchronously
4. Weigh strategic impact against time investment for each item
5. Identify which items deserve a dedicated deep-dive outside the weekly meeting
6. Redesign the meeting structure and time allocations based on the above

Before writing your final answer, work through your analysis in a scratchpad block. For each agenda item, reason through:
- How directly it maps to one or more of the stated objectives (and flag any item that doesn't map to any objective)
- Its decision criticality and the reasoning behind that rating
- Whether real-time executive discussion is actually required, or whether it's status-sharing that could be handled async
- A first-pass time allocation, checked against the other items so the total stays realistic for a single weekly meeting

<scratchpad>
[Your analysis here]
</scratchpad>

If the objectives or agenda items don't give you enough detail to judge an item's criticality or fit with confidence, say so explicitly and state your assumption rather than inventing a rationale that isn't supported by the input.

Before presenting your final answer, verify every recommendation ties back to a specific objective or agenda item provided above, that your time allocations sum to a realistic meeting length, and that your response follows the output format below exactly.

Provide your final recommendations in exactly this structure:

**KEEP IN MEETING (with time allocations):**
- Items that should remain in the weekly exec meeting, each with a specific time allocation in minutes, justified by decision criticality and strategic importance

**MOVE TO ASYNC:**
- Items that can be handled through email, shared documents, or other asynchronous methods, with reasoning for why they don't require real-time executive discussion

**DEEP DIVE SESSIONS:**
- Items that need a dedicated deep-dive outside the weekly meeting, with suggested frequency and format

**ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS:**
- Other structural changes to maximize strategic impact — meeting cadence, preparation requirements, or follow-up process suggestions

Ensure your time allocations add up to a realistic meeting duration and that items are prioritized by strategic impact and decision urgency.
```

---

## Pro tips
- Write objectives as outcomes, not activities — "cut CAC 15%" gives the model something to check alignment against; "improve marketing" doesn't
- Include the current time allocation for each agenda item if you have one, so the model has a baseline to cut from
- If two items both score "high criticality," the deep-dive recommendation is where to look first before assuming both need full meeting time
- Re-run monthly as objectives shift — an item that was a "keep" in month one can become a "move to async" once its underlying decision has been made

## Example use case
**Sunday night before your Monday exec sync:** Paste in Q3's three top objectives and your 5 proposed agenda items → get a redesigned 45-minute agenda with two items moved to async and one flagged for its own deep-dive session.
