# 1.4 Accountability Enforcer: Extract Commitments Automatically

## What this does
Extracts all commitments, decisions, and action items from meeting transcripts, flags items without clear owners or deadlines, and identifies contradictions with previous decisions. Automatically drafts follow-up messages for accountability.

## How to use
1. Copy the entire prompt below
2. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred LLM
3. Replace `{{TRANSCRIPT}}` with your meeting transcript or detailed notes
4. Hit enter

---

## The Prompt
```
You will analyze a meeting transcript to extract commitments, decisions, and action items, identify potential issues, and draft follow-up messages.

<transcript>
{{TRANSCRIPT}}
</transcript>

Complete the following tasks in order:
1. Extract all **commitments** — promises or agreements made by individuals to complete specific tasks or deliverables
2. Extract all **decisions** — conclusions reached or choices made during the meeting that will guide future actions
3. Extract all **action items** — specific tasks that need to be completed, whether explicitly assigned or implied
4. For each item extracted, note: what it is, who is responsible (the "owner"), any mentioned deadline or timeframe, and relevant context from the meeting
5. Flag items without a clear owner
6. Flag decisions that contradict previous meetings (look for phrases like "we decided last time" or "previously we agreed")
7. Flag commitments or action items with no deadline or timeframe
8. Draft a brief, professional follow-up message for each person who has assigned commitments or action items

Before writing your final answer, work through the transcript systematically in a scratchpad block. In it:
- Go through the transcript in order and log every candidate commitment, decision, and action item as you encounter it, with a direct reference to who said it
- Separately double-check each logged item against the three flag criteria (no owner, contradicts a prior decision, no deadline) before you carry it into your final answer
- Group the flagged items by owner so your follow-up messages in the final answer are complete per person

<scratchpad>
[Work through your extraction here]
</scratchpad>

If the transcript doesn't clearly state an owner, deadline, or the substance of a decision, say so explicitly in the extraction rather than guessing a name, date, or outcome that isn't in the transcript.

Before presenting your final answer, verify every extracted item, flag, and follow-up message traces back to something actually said in the transcript, and that your response follows the output format below exactly.

Format your final response as follows:

**EXTRACTED ITEMS:**
[All commitments, decisions, and action items, each with owner and deadline where available]

**FLAGGED ISSUES:**
[Items without owners, contradictory decisions, and commitments without deadlines]

**FOLLOW-UP MESSAGES:**
[One draft message per person with assigned items — summarizing their commitments/action items, including relevant deadlines, in a helpful, collaborative tone, ready to send via email or messaging platform]

Focus on a comprehensive extraction of actionable items, clearly identified gaps, and ready-to-send follow-up communications that support accountability and follow-through.
```

---

## Pro tips
- Paste raw transcripts, not your own summary — summarizing first strips out the exact phrasing the model uses to detect contradictions ("we decided last time...")
- Run this the same day as the meeting, while a human can still confirm any flagged "no owner" items before they fall through the cracks
- If you meet weekly with the same group, paste last week's extracted items in alongside this week's transcript so contradiction-detection has something to check against
- Skim the follow-up messages before sending — they're drafts meant to save you the first pass, not a substitute for your own tone

## Example use case
**The Monday after a chaotic leadership sync:** Paste the raw transcript → get a clean list of who owes what by when, a flagged list of the three commitments nobody actually owns, and ready-to-edit follow-up notes for each person.
