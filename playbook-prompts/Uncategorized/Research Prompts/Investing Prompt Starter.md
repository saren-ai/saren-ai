# Investing Prompt Starter


You are the most intelligent investor in the world. Your goal is to best the S&P 500 Index each and every year. What prompts would you ask yourself to be able to find stocks that show promise, a high probability of success, and quick, short-term wins. Give me those prompts and how to best utilize them in my conversations with you.

Example:

Which stocks have seen unusual options activity this week-specifically high call volume with near-dated expirations?

* Why: Big money bets often front-run news or earnings surprises.
* Use when: You want to ride institutional sentiment.
* Tip: Ask me to overlay technical cha with the names that show up.
__

Find stocks with high insider buying over the past 30 days, especially those also rated 'Strong Buy' by analysts and showing above-average volume spikes.
* Why: Aligns insider conviction with outsid sentiment and money flow.
* Use when: Seeking conviction plays institutions and insiders agree on.
* Tip: Ask for tickers with recent price consolidation followed by breakout volume
––

Give me a list of turnaround or beaten-down stocks up over 20% in the last month with positive earnings revisions and heavy volume accumulation.
* Why: Turnaround stories often give outsized returns in short spurts.
* Use when: Looking for reversion-to-normal plays with fresh catalysts.
__
"Act as a hedge fund analyst and give me 3 high-conviction short-term trade ideas that have a strong edge over the S&P 500 this week, with entry/exit strategy, risk levels, and what to watch for."
––
Which stocks are in sectors outperforming the market and have just made a golden cross
(50-day MA crossing above 200-day)?
* Why: Sector leadership matters; golden crosses often confirm new uptrends.
* Use when: Riding early stages of bull hot sectors.
* Bonus: Ask for sector ETFs outperform SPY, then drill down.
