# 20 Brainstorming Prompts


## Mind Mapping
*Visualise branching clusters.*
Our eco-shoe launch needs taglines. Build a central mind-map with core values, branch brand tones, audience niches, and channel hooks. Output a Markdown diagram for team riffing.

## Brainwriting 6-3-5
*Silently stack idea lists and brainstorm.*
We need three new loyalty perks. Facilitate a 6-3-5: six teammates, three ideas each, five rounds. Capture rows in a table and flag repeating themes for follow-up.

## Lotus Blossom
*Expand seeds into petals.*
Take "community fitness" as the core. Use a Lotus Blossom grid to radiate eight themes and their sub-ideas. Return the filled diagram using ASCIl boxes.

## Crazy 8s
*Rapid sketches spark variety.*
Generate eight onboarding screens for our app in eight minutes (textual sketches). Number 1-8 and give a one-sentence concept for each.

## Round Robin
*Pass ideas in a circle.*
For a VR museum exhibit, run a Round Robin: start with a base idea, add twists each pass. Document four loops and list the evolved concepts in order.

## Storyboarding
*Narrate user journeys visually.*
Storyboard a teen using our
budgeting app for a week. Outline six panels with brief captions, emoji mood cues, and any friction points noticed.

## Affinity Mapping
*Cluster brainstorm outputs.*
After our sticky-note dump on sustainable packaging group ideas into affinities (cost, materials, brand vibe.
Produce a list with category headers and counts.

## Random Word
*Inject uncommon stimuli.*
Use the random word "compass" to spark snack-brand ideas. List ten link-ups and describe the three strongest concepts in more detail.

## Morphological Matrix
*Mix attributes systematically.*
Brainstorm a smart kettle. Build a 4 x 4 morphological chart: heat source, sensors, materials, power.
Combine promising sets into three concept pitches.

## Starbursting
*Question every angle.*
Pop-up book café launch: create a starburst of
who/what/when/where/why/how questions. Answer the top five to surface idea gaps worth solving.

## Rapid Ideation
*Timed flood of thoughts.*
Provide 50 social-post ideas for Earth Day in five minutes. Bullet raw thoughts, then bold the ten worth polishing for the calendar.

## Role Storming
*Ideate as someone else.*
Brainstorm new airport seating while pretending you're a cat. List insights from that POV, then translate the quirky angles into three feasible prototypes.

## Provocation (Po)
*Push outrageous starts.*
Po: "Magazines dissolve after reading
Generate eight business
ideas triggered by that provocation, then rate each for viability and wow-factor.

## Synectics
*Merge unrelated elements.*
Combine "coral reef" with "online calendar" via synectics. Detail metaphor links and extract five novel feature ideas we could prototype.

## Lightning Decision Jam
*Solve fast, vote fast.*
Run an LJ on low newsletter clicks: list obstacles, flip into solutions, dot-vote top three, craft action steps-formatted as step-wise blocks for Monday's stand-up.

## Futuring Backwards
*Work back from vision.*
Assume we're famed for zero-carbon logistics in 2030. Map milestones backwards to today and highlight brainstorm arenas for the next quarter.

## World Café
*Host rotating dialogue tables.*
Plan a virtual World Café on remote culture. Draft three rounds of seed questions, rotation timing, and a method to synthesise collective insights quickly.

## Brain Netting
*Storm ideas online.*
Set up asynchronous "brain netting" for new brand mascots. Show the instruction post, digital board layout, and a timeline for three contribution waves.

## Picture Association
*Spark with imagery cues.*
Show five random Unsplash images. For each, derive two product ideas for urban cyclists Summarise the ten suggestions in a neat list for review.

## Reverse Brainstorming
*Find obstacles to fix.*
Instead of "How to improve checkout," ask "How to make checkout painful." List sabotage moves, then invert them into six actionable enhancements.
