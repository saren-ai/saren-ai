# Travel $ Prompts


1. Flight Hacks Based On Location
Prompt:
"I live in [insert country/city). Share the top 10 flight booking hacks that work specifically in my region to find the cheapest international and domestic flights without needing travel agents or popular flight search engines."

2. Cheapest Booking Strategy
Prompt:
"I'm planning to travel from [insert departure city. to [insert destination] around [insert month/date]. Act as a professional flight deal expert. Give me step-by-step strategies to find the cheapest flight possible including best days to book, alternate airports, tools, and timing tips, all without using Skyscanner or Google Flights."

3. Explore Alternate Routes
Prompt:
"Help me find the cheapest way to travel from [insert origin] to [insert destination] even if it includes hidden-city ticketing, long layovers, or nearby airports. I don't mind taking indirect routes as long as I save money."

4. Local Travel Agent Tricks
Prompt:
"Act like a local travel agent in [insert country.. What insider strategies do you use to get dirt-cheap flight tickets for clients especially on domestic or regional routes? Skip the obvious stuff."

5. Automate Your Future Bookings
Prompt:
"Create a prompt that I can reuse monthly to find the cheapest flights from [insert city] to any country under linsert budget. It should prioritize safety, affordability, and visa-free "access.”

6. Affordable Destination Travel Ideas
Prompt:
"I have a travel budget of linsert currency] for flights. Act like a travel deals expert and suggest the top 5 international destinations I can fly to from [insert city/country, under that budget including best time to book and hidden tips to save more."

7. Low-Cost International Trips
Prompt:
"I want to fly internationally from [insert country. but avoid expensive visa fees.
Suggest the top visa-free or visa-on-arrival countries with the cheapest flights from my location and best months to travel."
