# 🎬 Prompt 1 — “The Samurai’s Morning” (Naturalistic Cinematic Realism)

At sunrise in San Onofre, gentle light spills over long, rolling waves. A 24-year-old samurai stands poised on his longboard, wearing a white gi and dark hakama. His katana is tied at his waist with a red cord. The ocean reflects the orange-pink sky, while mist drifts along the horizon. The scene feels meditative — discipline and nature in harmony.

Cinematography:
Camera shot: wide establishing shot, eye level, slowly tracking alongside the surfer.
Lens: 35 mm spherical lens, natural handheld motion with light saltwater mist.
Lighting: soft golden key light from rising sun, balanced with cool blue shadows.
Mood: serene, contemplative, cinematic realism.

Actions:

The samurai steadies his balance, adjusting his footing with quiet precision.

He dips his hand toward the wave’s face, feeling the texture of the water.

A faint smile crosses his face as he rides smoothly toward the light.

Background Sound:
Low surf rumble, distant gulls, soft shakuhachi flute fading into sea breeze.
