# 🎬 Prompt 2 — “The Ninja at Night” (Stylized Neo-Noir Fantasy)


The Pacific glows under a silver moon at San Onofre. A 24-year-old ninja woman glides over dark water, dressed in a sleek black shinobi outfit with a crimson sash trailing behind her. The wave sparkles faintly with bioluminescence as she cuts through it, illuminated only by moonlight and the gleam of her short blade strapped across her back.

Cinematography:
Camera shot: low-angle tracking shot from water level following the surfer’s path.
Lens: anamorphic 2.0x, with cinematic lens flares and soft bokeh highlights.
Lighting: strong moonlight key, cool silver-blue palette with gentle mist.
Mood: mysterious, disciplined, graceful — a surf noir tone.

Actions:

The ninja crouches low, slicing her board sharply through the glowing wave.

She twists in a smooth 360 turn, water scattering in luminous trails.

She lands cleanly and vanishes behind the crest.

Background Sound:
Echoing taiko drum rhythm blending into the hush of breaking surf.
