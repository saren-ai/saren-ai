# 🎬 Prompt 3 — “Samurai Soul, Sunset Ride” (Retro 1970s Surf Film Aesthetic)

Late afternoon at San Onofre. A golden-hour glow paints the sky in amber and mauve. A young samurai in traditional garb rides his board with effortless style, sleeves fluttering like sails. His board is aged wood with Japanese brushwork etched along the rails. The waves roll smooth and slow, the whole scene tinted like vintage film stock.

Cinematography:
Camera shot: medium-wide drone shot from front, tracking backward as the surfer approaches.
Lens: 16 mm film emulation with light grain, soft focus, and vintage flare.
Lighting: warm, directional sunlight from low horizon; reflections glint across the surface.
Mood: nostalgic, cinematic, tranquil joy.

Actions:

The samurai performs a slow, elegant cutback.

He bows lightly toward the horizon as he exits the wave.

The final frame freezes with the sun behind him, evoking a classic surf poster.

Background Sound:
Retro surf guitar, analog hiss, distant laughter from the beach carried by the wind.
