# Managers & Leaders


1. DECISION-MAKING
I am proposing [decision] to [stakeholders]. What might be some potential objections, and how can I address them?
2. PROBLEM-SOLVING
I am facing [challenge] in my department. I have tried (solution]. Give me five unique ideas to address this challenge.
3. EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE
My employee's strengths are [list strengths]. What role would be best for her to transition to within the company. 

4. CRISIS MANAGEMENT
Our team is facing a crisis, such as [specific situation].
Provide a step-by-step plan to manage the situation and minimize damage.
5. COMMUNICATION SKILLS
I need to deliver difficult feedback to an employee about [issue]. Help me craft a message that is clear, empathetic, and actionable.
6. TEAM MOTIVATION
My team is feeling demotivated due to [reason].
Suggest five actionable ways to boost morale and re-energize the team.

6. DELEGATION AND EMPOWERMENT
I need to delegate [task/project] to my team. Help me identify the right person for the task and create a clear handover plan.
7. CHANGE MANAGEMENT
Our organization is undergoing a major change, such as [specific change]. Provide a communication plan to ensure transparency and buy-in from employees.

9. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Help me draft a constructive performance review for an employee who excels in [area] but needs improvement in [area].
