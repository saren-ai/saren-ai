# Analyze all of your competitors Google Ads with ChatGPT-4’s Code Interpreter


Download all your Search Ads, and all of the ads of your competitors. I used SEMRush ($) for this. Your agency probably has this tool, and you can request—ask them to put it all in one CSV. 

Here are the columns you’ll likely have to work with:

Company | Ad Headline | Ad Description | Visible URL | URL

Digital Marketers will recognize these as the basic fields in Google Ads.

I did this for my current client who is in the Application Security space and amongst of competitive set of 10 I compiled a CSV with 1,486 ads. All of which are live right now, so they are in the public domain.

Once you upload them into Code Interpreter, here are a few of the responses (and the graphs) that ChatGPT provided me.

1. Give me a count of how many ads each company is currently running.
2. Can you show me that as a bar graph?
3. Can you ad the count to each of the bars on the graph?
4. Best practice is to include the keyword you are targeting in both the ad headline and the ad description. What keywords or phrases are most commonly used in the headlines and descriptions of ads within our competitive set?
5. How many ads is each company running that includes their brand name in the ad itself. Graph the number for each of the companies in the data, and include the count on the bar within the graph.
6. Combine the two graphs so far to show the total number of ads each company is running, and the number of ads with their brand name. Make this a segmented bar graph with the numbers within each bar.
7. Redo the graph to show companies in descending order from left to right. 
8. Which calls to action are most used in the ads? 
9. Please graph this as a bar chart in descending order from left to right.
10. What are common themes, emotional triggers or value propositions in the headlines and descriptions of the ads?
11. Are there patterns in the length of ad headlines or descriptions?
12. How do our (Qwiet.ai) headlines and descriptions compare to our competitors' in terms of keyword usage and messaging?
13. Are there trends in the language or tone used in ad copy in this competitive set (e.g., formal, informal, emotive, etc.)?
14. Can any seasonal trends be identified in the ad copy (e.g., references to holidays, events, seasons, etc.)?
15. Can any timely Cybersecurity trends be identified in the ad copy (e.g., references to specific named Cybersecurity attacks, attack vectors, attack surfaces, etc.)?
16. What are the most relevant, but uncommon, keywords among this data from the Application Security competitive set—and therefore an opportunity to use to set ourselves apart?
17. Provide a list of Acronyms from all the ads and a count of their appearance, by brand. Ignore the company information, provide a full list of all the acronyms, their meaning, and the overall count in a table.
18. How many different landing pages do each of the companies in the table have, compared to the number of ads? Use the column URL. 
19. What are the most relevant, but uncommon, keywords among this data from the Application Security competitive set—and therefore an opportunity to use to set ourselves apart?
20. Provide a complete list of ad headlines targeting developers and engineers
21. Provide a full list of all the acronyms in this file, their meaning, and the overall count in a table.
22. How many different landing pages do each of the companies in the table have, compared to the number of ads? Set up the table with the following columns: Company | Ad Count | Landing Page Count | Ratio, calculated as Ad Count divided by Landing Page Count, for each company.
23. How would you structure the UTM parameters to accompany this file naming convention.
