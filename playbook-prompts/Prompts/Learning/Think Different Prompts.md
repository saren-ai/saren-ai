# Think Different Prompts

## 
## 1/ Teach me what I've ignored for too long

What critical skills, ideas, or mental models do high performers wish they'd learned earlier? Create a list of 10 l've likely missed and why each matters.

## 2/ Collapse a year of wisdom into an hour

As my personal mentor, compress the most important lessons from psychology, philosophy, and real-world experience into one power-packed session.

## 3/ Show me what school never did

Create a personal curriculum that teaches me to think clearly, act strategically, communicate persuasively, and adapt quickly. Include real-world applications.

## 4/ Decode how people become unstoppable

Explain the psychology, belief systems, and behaviors of someone who consistently breaks past limits, even when things get hard or boring.

## 5/ Teach me like I'm building a legacy

Show me the thinking that scales impact, influence, and income. Go beyond tactics to the mindset shifts required to lead at the highest level.

## 6/ Create mental models I can't unsee

Design 5 original mental models that simplify decision-making, evaluating people, learning from failure, and spotting leverage opportunities.

## 7/ Train me to be future-proof

What would a version of me 10 years ahead already know?
Create a training blueprint that upgrades my thinking to make me impossible to replace.
