# GSD Prompts for Work


Daily Task Planning
I have a list of tasks for today: List your tasks. Please prioritize them using the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent/important framework) and suggest the best order to complete them efficiently. Also, provide estimated time for each task and recommend any productivity techniques (like Pomodoro, batching, or time blocking) to maximize focus.

Proficient Email Writing
I need to write an email to [Recipient's Name/Position] about [Topic]. The purpose of the email is to inform, request, follow up, delegate, etc.J. Please structure the email using the BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) method, keeping it professional, concise, and clear. Make sure it includes a compelling subject line, a clear call to action, and an appropriate closing.

Meeting Preparation
I am organizing a meeting on [Date] at [Time] with [Attendees]. The goal of the meeting is to [Goal of the meeting].
Please create a structured agenda, ensuring each item has a time allocation and a responsible person. Also, suggest potential discussion questions and anticipated roadblocks. At the end, draft a follow-up email template summarizing the key decisions and action items.

Summarisation
I have a long document/report about [Topic. The key sections include List key sections if available. Please summarize the document in [X] bullet points, highlighting the most important insights, recommendations, and key takeaways.
Also, provide an executive summary in 3-5 sentences that I can quickly review before a meeting.
