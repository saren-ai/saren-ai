====

**Objective:**

This project aims to generate interactive visual insights and strategic foresight into the electric vehicle (EV) industry landscape through 2030. The goal is to inform high-level decision-making by the Board of Management with data-driven projections, competitor benchmarking, and macro-industry trends relevant to our product roadmap and market positioning.

####

**Visual Content Guidelines:**

- **Visual Types:**
    - Use **line charts** for showcasing trends over time (e.g., EV adoption, battery prices).
    - Use **bar charts** for **comparative metrics** (e.g., market share by brand, country policies).
    - Use **heat maps or choropleth maps** for **geographic distribution** (e.g., infrastructure rollout, demand hotspots).
    - Use **bubble charts or scatter plots** when showing correlation (e.g., range vs. price comparisons across models).
- **Design Standards:**
    - Ensure visualizations are **clean, minimalist, and boardroom-ready**.
    - Use our **corporate color palette** (Primary: Deep Blue; Accent: Silver Grey).
    - All visuals must include:
        - **Clear, descriptive titles**
        - **Legends and axis labels**
        - **Data labels where meaningful**
        - **Annotations for critical inflection points**

####

**Output Format for Each Visualization Task:**

1. **Interactive Artifact:**
    - Provide the visualization in a shareable, interactive format (e.g., Plotly, Tableau, Power BI, etc.)
2. **Executive Summary (3–5 Bullet Points):**
    - Highlight **key insights** relevant to strategy, investment decisions, and product direction.
    - Use **clear, concise** language suitable for executive review.
3. **Methodology Notes:**
    - List **data sources** (e.g., IEA, BloombergNEF, Statista, internal sales data).
    - Note **assumptions or modeling techniques** used (e.g., CAGR calculations, scenario planning).
    - Briefly explain **any limitations** or data gaps.

####

**Scope of Visual Analysis (Examples):**

- Global EV adoption rates (2015–2030, projected)
- Regional breakdown of EV incentives and policy mandates
- Competitor EV model rollout and production targets
- Charging infrastructure availability by market
- Battery tech innovation timeline and cost per kWh projections
- Consumer sentiment and purchase intent surveys
