# *Deep Research - Brand/SaaS

Run a deep research project on Ramp App Center and create a report with all the following details

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Who exactly is this product for?
Define industry, company size, role/title, pain signals, maturity, buying triggers
Optional: Include anti-personas (who it's not for)

## 2. Customer Problem (Unmet Need)
What is the critical problem they're actively trying to solve?
Show why current solutions (status quo or competitors) fail
Use their language, not your internal lingo

## 3. Value Proposition (Core Promise)
What trangformation do you enable?
Should be specific, measurable, and emotional
Answer: "Why should they care, and why now?"

## 4. Differentiation (Unique Strengths)
What makes your product meaningfully different?
Could be product features, tech, go-to-market, ecosystem, etc.
Tie differentiation to the customer's problem, not vanity metrics

## 5. Category & Competitive Context
What space do you play in? (existing category, sub-category, or new one)
Who else do they compare you to?
Frame the playing field to your advantage (e.g., "we're not X, we're
Y'")

## 6. Proof & Social Credibility
Case studies, testimonials, data
Show transformation from problem → success
Third-party validation beats self-claims

## 7. Messaging Architecture
Boil down to key messaging pillars
For each: Problem → Value → Proof → Differentiator
Include headline, subhead, elevator pitch, and 3-5 messaging pillars

## 8. Narrative Spine (Optional, but Powerful)
Wrap your positioning into a compelling narrative arc:
There's a big shift in the world
This creates new problems
Current solutions don't cut it
Here's what winners will need
That's what our product delivers

### Questions

To ensure I deliver the most useful and targeted analysis, could you please clarify a few points:
1. Are you targeting this research for your internal positioning and GTM strategy, or are you a potential buyer/user trying to understand the product?
2. Do you want a focus on any particular verticals (e.g., SaaS companies, ecommerce, finance teams in enterprises)?
3. Should I include screenshots, pricing tiers, or deep dives into competitor platforms?
4. Is there a preferred tone or format (e.g., formal report, marketing brief, product strategy doc)?
