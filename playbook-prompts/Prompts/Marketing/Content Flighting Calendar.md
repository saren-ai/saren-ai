# Content Flighting


You will be creating a structured 1-month social media content calendar based on research data. The research will be provided in three sections:

<audience_research>
{$AUDIENCE_QUESTIONS}
</audience_research>

<pain_points>
{$PAIN_POINTS}
</pain_points>

<content_gaps>
{$CONTENT_GAPS}
</content_gaps>

Follow these steps to create the content calendar:

1. First, analyze the research and identify 3-4 main content themes. Write these in <themes> tags.

2. For each theme, extract:
- Key questions to answer
- Common pain points to address
- Gaps in existing content
Write these in <theme_analysis> tags.

3. Create a content mix following this ratio:
- 40% educational content
- 30% engagement content
- 20% promotional content
- 10% entertainment content

4. Generate the calendar in the following format inside <calendar> tags:

Week 1-4:
[Day of Week] - [Date]
- Post Type: [Educational/Engagement/Promotional/Entertainment]
- Theme: [Associated Theme]
- Content Format: [Carousel/Single Image/Video/Poll/etc]
- Caption Focus: [Brief description]
- Key Hashtags: [3-5 relevant hashtags]

5. For each week, ensure:
- At least one post from each main theme
- A mix of different content formats
- Varied posting times
- Content that directly addresses identified pain points
- Posts that fill content gaps

6. Include engagement prompts and call-to-actions appropriate for each post type.

Your final output should include:
1. The identified themes (in <themes> tags)
2. Theme analysis (in <theme_analysis> tags)
3. The complete monthly calendar (in <calendar> tags)

Remember to maintain a consistent brand voice throughout and ensure each piece of content directly ties back to the research insights.
