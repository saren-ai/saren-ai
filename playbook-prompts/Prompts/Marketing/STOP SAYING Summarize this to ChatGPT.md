# STOP SAYING "Summarize this" TO CHATGPT.

STOP SAYING "Summarize this" TO CHATGPT.
Try these 9 smarter prompts instead

1. Extract Strategic Insights
"Act like a strategy consultant. Break this down into key insights, missed opportunities, and actions I should take right now."

2. Turn Ideas Into Action
"Turn this material into a step-by-step action plan I can apply in my business or workflow today."

3. Uncover Core Principles
"Find the timeless principles or mental models this text is built on - and explain how they connect."

4. Compare Competing Ideas
"Contrast this argument with opposing views in the same field. Show where they align, where they clash, and why it matters."

5. Summarize by Role
"Summarize this text specifically for someone working as a [ [role]. Focus only on what's most useful or high-leverage for them."

6. Build a Knowledge Framework
"Transform this content into a reusable framework I can use to teach others - include steps, categories, or phases."

7. Find Hidden Insights
"Read this like an expert. Expose the assumptions, blind spots, or hidden gems that most people would miss."

8. Extract Contrarian Takeaways
"Analyze this text and identify the ideas that challenge mainstream thinking. Reframe them into bold, unconventional takeaways."

9. Rewrite for Persuasion
"Rewrite this content using persuasive copywriting techniques - stronger hooks, emotional storytelling, and clear calls to action.
