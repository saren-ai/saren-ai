# Prompt: Google Ads Campaign


I would like to create some copy for three new landing pages. I will provide a PDF with information from our Services team, and 3 specific keyword phrases, one at a time. 

You will provide the following campaign components.

I. Landing Page copy. The Landing Page should have the following elements:

1. A headline that includes the keyword phrase itself.
2. A subhead that give a little more colorful description
3. A paragraph that explains the Qweit AI Services offering, relative to the keyword 
4. Three primary bullet points that give some details to our solution.
5. A final paragraph that encourages the visitor to fill out a contact us form as a CTA.
6. A <155 character META description of the Landing Page

II. Google Search Ads Copu

1. Ten (10) Google Ad headlines, each <30 characters in length.
2. 4 Google Ads descriptions, for each individual keyword phrase. 

III. UTM Parameters, name the campaign, and the utm_term

1. utm_source=google&
2. utm_medium=ppc&
3. utm_content=appsec&
4. utm_campaign={campaign}&
5. utm_term={keyword}

IV. Write an immediate follow up thank you email to be sent to the user who fills out the form.

1. Subject One
2. Paragraph
3. Three links to something that will bring back the user to our site again
