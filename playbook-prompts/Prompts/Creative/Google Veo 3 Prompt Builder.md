# Google Veo 3 Prompt Builder


Write me a detailed prompt for Google's new Veo 3 Al video generator that makes videos with sound. My idea is [explain the idea]. The prompt should follow this structure:

1. Scene: Describe the scene location in detail
2. Style: Describe the look and feel, the visual style of the video.
3. Character: Who's in the shot? Both the main character and any other key people.
4. Action: What are the main characters doing?
5. Dialogue: What are the main characters saying?
6. Camera direction: Is the camera moving? Is it handheld? Is it zooming in or out? Panning?
7. Sound: Any background music or effects.
